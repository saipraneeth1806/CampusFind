A score is a suggestion, not a probability

A higher score does not prove that two reports refer to the same item or that anyone owns it. Contact the poster through CampusFind’s private inbox and arrange the handover in a public, staffed place.

Privacy and safety

  • Keep descriptions general. Hold back one identifying detail for verification.
  • Do not post personal phone numbers, home addresses, passwords, or sensitive identifiers.
  • Use CampusFind’s private inbox to contact the poster. Email addresses are not displayed on reports.
  • Use campus-approved channels to arrange a return. Choose a public, staffed place.
  • This portfolio demo has no email verification, password recovery, rate limiting, or moderation. Do not use it for urgent or sensitive matters.
